Questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does a notary visit cost?

General notarization is $10 per notarial act, plus a mobile travel fee from $35. Document signing packages (will, power of attorney and similar) are $350. We always confirm your total before you pay.

What ID do I need for notarization?

A current government-issued photo ID, such as a driver's license, state ID or passport. The name on your ID should match the name on your document.

How does remote online notarization work?

We meet by secure video call. You show your ID, sign electronically, and receive your notarized document by email. You need a phone or computer with a camera and your photo ID.

Do you travel to me?

Yes. We cover Miami-Dade, Broward and West Palm Beach. Mobile visits are Monday and Tuesday, 8 AM to 8 PM, and at other times by request.

When are you available?

In person Monday and Tuesday, 8 AM to 8 PM. Online Wednesday through Sunday, 7 AM to 12 PM. Rush and after-hours appointments by request.

What should I bring for tax preparation?

A photo ID, Social Security cards or ITIN letters for everyone on the return, your W-2s and 1099s, last year's return if you have it, and your bank details for direct deposit. Self-employed? Bring your income and expense records.

Is my information kept private?

Yes. We collect only what we need to serve you, we never sell your information, and we keep the records the law requires. See our privacy policy for details.
