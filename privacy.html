Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026 · Versión en español

L & S Tax and Notary Services LLC ("L & S", "we") respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Contact details you share with us, such as your name, phone number and email address, when you book an appointment, message us or email us.
  • Details about the service you ask for, so we can prepare for your appointment.
  • Documents you ask us to notarize, translate or help prepare. We use them only to perform the service you requested.
  • Payment information. Payments are processed by our payment providers (such as Stripe, Zelle, Venmo or Cash App). We never see or store your full card number.

How we use your information

  • To answer your questions and confirm and perform your appointments.
  • To process payments and send receipts.
  • To keep the records that Florida notary law requires, including our notary journal, and the records that tax preparation rules require.

What we never do

We never sell your personal information, and we never share it for someone else's marketing.

Who sees your information

Only the people and services needed to serve you: our payment processors, our booking and communication tools, WhatsApp when you choose to message us there, and government offices when a filing requires it.

How long we keep it

Notary records are kept as long as Florida law requires. Tax records are kept as IRS rules require. Other information is kept only as long as needed to serve you.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete what the law does not require us to keep. Email info@lstaxnotary.com or call (305) 707-8504.

A note on sensitive documents

Please do not send documents with sensitive details through WhatsApp or the website chat until we ask for them. We will tell you the safest way to share what your service needs.
